Trademark Policy

Introduction

This is the trademark policy for TeapotLang and its associated project materials.

TeapotLang’s source code is available under a free and open source software license. However, that license does not grant permission to use TeapotLang’s trademarks.

This policy explains how you may use the TeapotLang name and associated marks. The goal is to balance two things:

1. TeapotLang’s need to ensure that its trademarks remain reliable indicators of the software and projects officially maintained by the TeapotLang project.
2. The community’s ability to use, modify, distribute, and contribute to TeapotLang.

Our trademarks

This policy covers the name TeapotLang, the names of official TeapotLang projects, and associated logos, designs, trade dress, and other branding used to identify the TeapotLang project (collectively, the “Marks”).

In general

Whenever you use the Marks, you must do so in a way that does not mislead people about the source of the software.

For example, you may not describe a modified version of TeapotLang as simply being the official TeapotLang software, because doing so could cause users to believe they are receiving the same software distributed by the TeapotLang project.

You also may not use the TeapotLang Marks on a website, product, or service in a way that suggests that the TeapotLang project officially operates, endorses, sponsors, or approves that website, product, or service when it does not.

You may, however, truthfully state that you:

* Use TeapotLang.
* Contribute to the TeapotLang project.
* Participate in the TeapotLang community.
* Distribute an unmodified version of TeapotLang.
* Have created software that works with or uses TeapotLang.
* Have written documentation, tutorials, books, or other educational material about TeapotLang.

In addition:

* You may not use or register the Marks, in whole or in part, as your own trademark, service mark, domain name, company name, trade name, product name, or service name.
* You may not use an obvious variation, phonetic equivalent, foreign-language equivalent, abbreviation, or takeoff of the Marks for a similar or compatible product or service where that use could cause confusion with TeapotLang.
* You do not acquire ownership rights in the Marks through your use of them.
* Any goodwill generated through proper use of the Marks benefits the TeapotLang project.

Distribution of unmodified source code or executable code

When you redistribute an unmodified copy of TeapotLang, you are not changing the quality or nature of the software.

Therefore, you may retain the TeapotLang Marks included with the official distribution when redistributing an unmodified copy of the software.

This permission applies only to official TeapotLang distributions that have not been modified.

Distribution of executable code that you have compiled, or modified code

You may use the TeapotLang word mark to truthfully describe the origin of software that you provide, including modified versions of TeapotLang.

For example, you may state:

“This software is derived from TeapotLang.”

However, you may not use TeapotLang logos or other graphical Marks to imply that your modified version is an official TeapotLang release.

If you make substantive modifications to TeapotLang, you may identify your modified version using your own trademarks, logos, and branding.

You may continue to refer to TeapotLang to accurately describe the software’s origin, but your branding should make it clear that the modified version is not an official TeapotLang release.

You may use the TeapotLang Marks for distributions that are built from official TeapotLang source code when modifications are limited to:

* Enabling or disabling features already included in TeapotLang.
* Translations into other languages.
* Minor bug-fix patches.

Use of the TeapotLang Marks is not permitted for further modifications that materially change the software without making it clear that the resulting software is not an official TeapotLang distribution.

Statements about your software’s relationship with TeapotLang

You may use the TeapotLang word mark to truthfully describe the relationship between your software and TeapotLang.

The TeapotLang name should be used in a way that clearly describes the relationship.

Examples of acceptable wording include:

* “[Your software] uses TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] is powered by TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] runs on TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] is designed for use with TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] is compatible with TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] is an extension for TeapotLang.”
* “[Your software] is derived from TeapotLang.”

Avoid wording such as:

* “[Your software] TeapotLang”
* “Official TeapotLang [your software]”
* “TeapotLang [your software]”

when such wording could imply that your software is officially produced, maintained, or endorsed by the TeapotLang project.

Official TeapotLang projects

Only software, websites, repositories, and other projects explicitly identified by the TeapotLang project as official may use branding that implies official TeapotLang status.

Community projects are welcome to use the TeapotLang name descriptively, provided that their use follows this policy and does not imply official endorsement.

Questions about trademark use

If you are unsure whether a particular use of the TeapotLang Marks is permitted, choose wording and branding that clearly distinguishes your project from the official TeapotLang project.

When in doubt, contact the TeapotLang project maintainers before using the Marks in a way that could imply an official relationship.
